OnBrand Recorder

An internal companion app for the soundcore Work voice recorder, used by our team on Android and macOS.

What it does

  • Connects directly to the recorder over Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi and copies recordings to your phone or Mac.
  • Transcribes recordings on the device with Whisper.
  • Lets you tag recordings by project, search transcripts and export audio, text, subtitles or Word documents.
  • Optionally uploads recordings and transcripts to a folder in your own Google Drive.

Google Drive access

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app requests only the drive.file permission. It can create and update the folder and files it makes for your recordings and cannot see any other files in your Drive.

The app is distributed privately to our team and is not affiliated with Anker or soundcore.
