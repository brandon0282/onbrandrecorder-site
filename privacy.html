Privacy policy

OnBrand Recorder · Last updated October 25, 2026

OnBrand Recorder ("the app") is an internal tool for managing recordings from a soundcore Work voice recorder. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

Data stored on your device

Recordings, transcripts, project tags and settings are stored locally on your phone or Mac. We do not operate a server and do not receive copies of this data.

Transcription

By default, transcription runs entirely on your device with Whisper; audio does not leave the device. If you choose to enable an optional cloud transcription provider (Soniox or MOSS) with your own API key, audio is sent to that provider for transcription under its own privacy terms.

AI summaries

When AI summaries are enabled and you request a summary, the transcript text (not audio) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate it. No data is sent unless you request a summary.

Google user data

If you connect Google Drive, the app requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope and your basic Google account profile (name and email) to show which account is connected. With this scope the app can only access files and folders it creates. It uses this access solely to create a recordings folder in your Drive and to upload, update, rename and move the audio and transcript files you choose to sync. It does not read, download or delete any other files.

Google user data is used only to provide this upload feature. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not transferred to anyone else, and not used to train AI models. Sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure storage. You can disconnect at any time in the app (Settings → Google Drive → Sign out) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions; files already uploaded remain in your Drive under your control.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
